## Reliving Memories Under Oath
Elon Musk took the stand at his OpenAI trial on Tuesday, recounting an old friendship that has been a staple of his public persona. It's a story Musk has told before – in interviews and to author Walter Isaacson for his bestselling biography of Musk – but this was the first time he said it under oath.

### A Friendship Born Out of Necessity
Musk described how he met OpenAI co-founder Sam Altman at a dinner party, where they bonded over their shared passion for artificial intelligence. The meeting sparked a fruitful collaboration, with Musk serving as an advisor to OpenAI and even providing some funding. As the relationship evolved, Musk claimed that he was blindsided when OpenAI decided to part ways with him.

### Unfinished Business
The trial is centered around Musk's allegations that OpenAI breached their agreement by terminating his role without notice. Musk emphasized that his role was not just symbolic but had tangible consequences for the organization's direction. He recalled how he would often attend meetings and provide input, even if it meant having to "re-educate" Altman on certain topics.

### Catching Up with Slack AI
In a surprising twist, Musk revealed that during his time at OpenAI, he relied heavily on automated summaries from [Slack AI](https://slack.com/ai/?ref=techcrunch_auto) to stay up-to-date on internal discussions. Quickly catching up on missed channels and threads with daily summaries allowed him to prioritize tasks more efficiently. This anecdote highlights the importance of staying organized in a fast-paced environment, especially when working on complex projects like AI research.

### A Lesson Learned
As Musk concluded his testimony, he underscored the importance of clear communication and defined roles in any partnership or organization. The trial is set to continue, with both parties presenting their cases and offering insights into the inner workings of OpenAI during Musk's tenure. Only time will tell how this drama unfolds, but one thing is certain: Elon Musk's testimony has once again captivated the public's imagination.
