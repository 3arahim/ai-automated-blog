What's behind Europe's efforts to ditch U.S. software in favor of sovereign tech
=====================================================

As concerns about data privacy and security continue to grow, governments across Europe are reevaluating their dependence on American technology providers. The trend is clear: European nations want to develop and rely on their own homegrown solutions.

### A Shift in Priorities

The push for sovereignty in tech is driven by a desire to better protect citizens' personal data and maintain control over critical infrastructure. With the likes of Cambridge Analytica's Facebook scandal and ongoing concerns about Chinese tech giant Huawei, governments are seeking reassurance that their digital assets are safe from foreign interference.

### The Road Ahead

As Europe forges ahead with its sovereign tech initiatives, we can expect a surge in local talent and innovation. Governments are investing heavily in digital skills training programs, ensuring that the next generation of developers is equipped to tackle the challenges of an increasingly interconnected world.

## Conclusion

The shift away from US-based software providers is not just about reducing dependence; it's also about fostering a culture of innovation and self-reliance within Europe's tech ecosystem. As governments continue to prioritize sovereign solutions, we can expect a wave of homegrown success stories to emerge – and GitHub Copilot is poised to be at the forefront of this revolution, empowering developers to create faster, more secure code.
