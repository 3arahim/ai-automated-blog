## Critical Infrastructure Giant Itron Hit by Hack

Itron, a leading provider of smart utility meters and water and energy monitoring solutions to hundreds of millions of homes and businesses worldwide, has confirmed that it was the victim of a cyberattack. The company's critical infrastructure services are used daily by households, industries, and utilities to manage resources and ensure a stable supply chain.

### Itron's Response to the Incident

In a statement, Itron acknowledged the hack, stating that it is working closely with law enforcement and cybersecurity experts to contain and remediate the incident. The company has assured its customers that their data remains secure and that no sensitive information was compromised during the breach.
