The US Supreme Court appears split over controversial use of ‘geofence’ search warrants
=====================================

### A Delicate Balance

In a closely watched case, the US Supreme Court is expected to rule on whether law enforcement agencies can use "geofence" search warrants to identify criminal suspects by dragnet searching the databases of tech giants. The issue has sparked intense debate over the limits of government surveillance and the protection of privacy in the digital age.

### A New Frontier for Surveillance

Geofencing technology allows police to gather data on individuals who were near a specific location at a particular time, potentially linking them to a crime. While proponents argue that this tool can help solve crimes more efficiently, critics contend that it infringes upon fundamental rights and could lead to wrongful convictions.

### The High-Stakes Battle

As the Court deliberates, concerns are growing about the potential consequences of allowing such searches. "If we allow geofence warrants to be used without limits, we'll see a significant erosion of our privacy," warns civil liberties advocate, Jennifer Stahlman. On the other hand, law enforcement officials argue that this technology is a valuable tool in their fight against crime.
