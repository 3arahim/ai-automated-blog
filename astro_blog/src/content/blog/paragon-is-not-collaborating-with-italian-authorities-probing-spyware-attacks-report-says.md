## Paragon's Alleged Lack of Cooperation Raises Concerns

A recent report has cast a shadow over the alleged involvement of Israeli-American spyware maker Paragon in hacking attacks targeting Italian journalists and activists. The company had initially promised to assist authorities in determining what happened, but it appears that Paragon has not responded to requests for information.

### A Pattern of Uncooperativeness?

This latest development only adds fuel to the fire surrounding Paragon's reputation. Critics have long accused the company of being evasive when faced with scrutiny. In this case, Italian authorities are said to be seeking answers about the spyware attacks that compromised sensitive data and compromised the security of those targeted.
