## Hacker Who Allegedly Carried Out Cyberattacks for China is Extradited to U.S.

Xu Zewei, a 36-year-old Chinese national, has been extradited from Belgium to the United States to face charges related to his alleged involvement in a Chinese government hacking group. The group, known as APT41, is accused of carrying out widespread cyberattacks on thousands of US organizations and stealing COVID-19-related research.

### A Global Manhunt

According to court documents, Xu Zewei was part of the group that targeted over 13,000 victims in the United States, including universities, hospitals, and companies. The attacks were allegedly carried out between 2012 and 2021, with the hackers stealing sensitive information, including COVID-19-related research.

As authorities worked to track down Xu Zewei, they discovered that he had used his knowledge of coding languages like Python and C++ to evade detection. "He was a master of disguising his digital footprints," said a US official. "It took our team months of intense investigation to finally pinpoint his location in Belgium."
