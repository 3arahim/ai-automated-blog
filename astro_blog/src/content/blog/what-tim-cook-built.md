What Tim Cook Built
=====================

When Tim Cook took the reins at Apple in 2011, the company was already a global powerhouse. But under his leadership, Apple has continued to evolve and thrive, transforming into a tech giant that dominates multiple markets.

One of the most significant changes under Cook's tenure is Apple's growing commitment to social responsibility. From reducing its carbon footprint to diversifying its workforce, Apple has made significant strides in making its operations more sustainable and equitable. As [YOUR_AFFILIATE_LINK_HERE] highlights, this shift is reflected in the company's 2020 Supplier Responsibility Progress Report, which details Apple's efforts to promote fair labor practices throughout its supply chain.

Another key area of focus for Cook has been expanding Apple's services segment. Today, services like Apple Music, Apple TV+, and Apple Arcade generate billions of dollars in revenue each year. This shift towards services has allowed Apple to diversify its revenue streams and provide a more consistent flow of income.

However, with Tim Cook set to step down as CEO, John Ternus will be taking the reins at a time when the tech landscape is rapidly changing. One major challenge he'll face is navigating the ongoing chip shortage, which has had a significant impact on Apple's manufacturing processes. Another area of concern will be the increasingly competitive nature of the smartphone market, with rivals like Samsung and Google pushing hard to gain ground.

Despite these challenges, Ternus will have the advantage of building on Cook's legacy, which has laid the groundwork for future growth and innovation at Apple. As we look ahead to what John Ternus will build upon, it's clear that Apple is well-positioned to continue driving technological advancements and shaping the future of the tech industry.
