## China Blocks Meta's $2B Manus Deal After Months-Long Probe

China has ordered Meta to unwind its multibillion-dollar Manus acquisition, dealing a potential setback to Mark Zuckerberg's push into AI agents. The move comes after a months-long probe by Chinese regulators, who reportedly raised concerns over national security and the potential impact on the domestic tech industry.

### Background on Manus Acquisition

Meta's $2 billion acquisition of Manus was announced in September 2022, with the aim of expanding its presence in the artificial intelligence (AI) space. The acquisition was seen as a significant move for Meta, which has been looking to diversify its business beyond social media and online advertising.

### Concerns Over National Security

However, Chinese regulators have now ordered Meta to unwind the deal, citing concerns over national security and the potential impact on the domestic tech industry. It is unclear what specific issues prompted these concerns, but it is likely that regulators were worried about the potential for Manus's AI technology to be used in a way that could harm China's interests.

### Impact on Meta's Push into AI

The blocking of the deal is a significant setback for Meta's push into AI, which has been seen as a key area of growth for the company. With this acquisition no longer going ahead, it remains to be seen how Meta will proceed with its plans in this space.
