## A New Path Forward

In a surprise announcement, Zap Energy revealed that it would be expanding its product lineup to include nuclear fission reactors. This news sent shockwaves through the clean energy community, as many had been following the startup's progress in developing fusion technology.

### Fusion and Fission: What's the Difference?

While both fusion and fission reactions involve manipulating atomic nuclei, they operate on different principles. Fusion reactions combine atomic nuclei to release energy, whereas fission reactions split them apart to release energy. In the context of Zap Energy's pivot, the company will be leveraging its expertise in nuclear physics to develop more efficient and safer fission reactors.

### The Benefits of Fission

Zap Energy's decision to add fission reactors to its portfolio is not without reason. Nuclear fission has the potential to provide a significant increase in energy output compared to traditional fossil fuel-based power plants. Additionally, fission reactors can be designed to operate at a lower cost and with improved safety features compared to earlier generations of nuclear reactors.
