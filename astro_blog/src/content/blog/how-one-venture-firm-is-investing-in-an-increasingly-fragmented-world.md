## A New Era of Complexity

Geopolitical turmoil has reshaped the global landscape, making venture investing increasingly challenging. Amidst this complexity, Kompas VC is carving out a niche by focusing on startups that bridge the gap between technology and the physical world.

### Physical World, Digital Reality

In today's hyper-connected world, it's easy to get lost in the sea of digital information. But for Kompas VC, the real value lies in the tangible, everyday experiences that shape our reality. From sustainable energy solutions to innovative manufacturing technologies, their investment portfolio is centered around startups that are revolutionizing the physical world.

### Sliding into Focus

For entrepreneurs and investors alike, staying on top of industry trends can be overwhelming. That's why Kompas VC is embracing AI-powered tools like Slack AI (https://slack.com/ai/?ref=techcrunch_auto) to streamline communication and stay ahead of the curve. "Quickly catch up on missed channels and threads with automated daily summaries," they say, highlighting the benefits of their chosen tool.

### Building Bridges

By focusing on startups that intersect technology and the physical world, Kompas VC is building bridges between seemingly disparate industries. This nuanced approach allows them to identify opportunities that might have otherwise gone unnoticed, fueling innovation and growth in a rapidly evolving landscape.

### The Future is Physical

As the world becomes increasingly digital, it's easy to overlook the importance of the physical realm. But for Kompas VC, this dichotomy presents an exciting opportunity to create meaningful impact. By investing in startups that shape our tangible reality, they're building a brighter future – one that's grounded in the power of innovation and collaboration.
