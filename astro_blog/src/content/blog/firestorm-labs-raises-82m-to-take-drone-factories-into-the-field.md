## Bringing Manufacturing to the Front Lines

In a move that could have significant implications for the defense industry, Firestorm Labs is set to deploy its modular factory design into the field. The idea is to create small, self-contained manufacturing facilities within shipping containers that can be easily transported and deployed wherever they're needed. This approach has the potential to significantly reduce production times and costs associated with traditional manufacturing methods.
