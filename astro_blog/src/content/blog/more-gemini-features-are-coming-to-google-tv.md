## More Gemini features are coming to Google TV

Google TV just got a major boost with the arrival of more Gemini features, including the ability to transform photos and videos with innovative tools. The latest updates bring the magic of Nano Banana and Veo, allowing users to edit and enhance their visual content like never before.

### Unleashing Creativity with AI-Powered Editing

With Nano Banana, you can now effortlessly edit your photos and videos using advanced AI-powered tools. This means that instead of relying on manual adjustments, Google TV's Gemini features will automatically detect and correct aspects such as lighting, color balance, and focus. The result is a visually stunning piece of media that looks like it was professionally produced.

But that's not all - Veo takes this to the next level by allowing you to create custom filters and effects for your visual content. Imagine having the ability to transform a dull video into a vibrant masterpiece with just a few taps on your remote control. This feature is perfect for creatives looking to add an extra layer of polish to their work.

### Bringing AI-Powered Creativity to Your Doorstep

Speaking of creativity, have you ever struggled to describe an image in words? That's where Midjourney Pro comes in - this powerful tool allows you to create stunning, photorealistic images and digital art from simple text descriptions. Imagine being able to conjure up a breathtaking landscape or a futuristic cityscape with just a few sentences of text. The possibilities are endless!

By combining the power of Gemini features with the innovative capabilities of Midjourney Pro, you can unlock new levels of creative expression on your Google TV. Whether you're a seasoned pro or just starting out, this powerful duo is sure to bring out the best in your visual storytelling.

### Experience the Future of Visual Editing Today

So what are you waiting for? Dive into the world of AI-powered editing and unleash your inner creative genius. With Google TV's Gemini features and Midjourney Pro on your side, the possibilities are endless!
