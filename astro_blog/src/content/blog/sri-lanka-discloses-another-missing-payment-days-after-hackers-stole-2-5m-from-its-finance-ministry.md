## Sri Lanka Discloses Another Missing Payment After $2.5M Hack

The government of Sri Lanka has suffered yet another blow to its finances after a second cybersecurity incident in recent days, with officials revealing that an additional $1.5 million has gone missing. This latest development comes just days after hackers stole $2.5 million from the country's finance ministry.

### The Double Whammy

The double whammy of financial setbacks is likely to have significant implications for Sri Lanka's already-strained economy, which has been grappling with the aftermath of a debt crisis that peaked in 2022. With the country still recovering from those difficulties, these latest incidents are set to further complicate its economic landscape.
