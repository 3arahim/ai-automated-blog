This impressive feat is expected to revolutionize the field of artificial intelligence (AI) and its applications.

### A New Era in AI

The British AI lab's mission is to create an AI that learns without relying on human data, which has been a significant challenge for the industry. Silver, who was previously a researcher at DeepMind, has brought his expertise to Ineffable Intelligence to tackle this problem. The $1.1 billion funding will be used to support the development of this innovative technology.
