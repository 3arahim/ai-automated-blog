## Founder of Shark Tank-Backed Startup Scholly Sues Acquirer Sallie Mae Over Wrongful Termination and Allegations of Student Data Selling

Chris Gray, the founder of Scholly, a startup that helps students find scholarships and financial aid, is suing his acquirer, Sallie Mae, for wrongful termination. The suit also alleges that Sallie Mae is selling student data through its subsidiary, Franklin Resources.

In a statement, Gray said he was terminated without cause in October 2020 after Scholly's acquisition by Sallie Mae. He claims that Sallie Mae has failed to honor the terms of the deal and has instead used his company as a means to sell student data.

Sallie Mae has denied the allegations, stating that it is committed to protecting student privacy and has strict protocols in place to ensure data security. The company also vows to fight Gray's lawsuit.

Scholly was founded by Gray in 2015 after he struggled to find scholarships as a college student. The startup quickly gained traction and was acquired by Sallie Mae in 2020. However, the acquisition has been marred by controversy over the handling of student data.
