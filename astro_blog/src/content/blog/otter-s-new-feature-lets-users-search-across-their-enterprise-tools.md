## Otter Unleashes New Search Feature Across Enterprise Tools

Otter, the AI-powered note-taking app, is rolling out a game-changing feature that enables users to search across multiple enterprise tools. This move aims to simplify the way professionals organize and retrieve information from various applications, streamlining their workflow in the process.

### Seamless Integration for Enhanced Productivity

With Otter's new search feature, users can now easily access and combine notes, tasks, and files from various platforms like Slack, Trello, Asana, and more. This integration means that professionals no longer have to jump between multiple apps or manually copy and paste information to get the insights they need.

### Meeting Notes Made Easy

In addition to the search feature, Otter is also releasing a new Windows app that can capture meeting notes without users having to join the meeting themselves. This innovative solution uses AI-powered transcription technology to automatically generate meeting summaries, freeing up valuable time for more important tasks.

"As professionals increasingly rely on digital tools to stay organized and productive, we're excited to provide solutions that simplify their workflow," said Otter's CEO. "By combining our search feature with automatic meeting note-taking capabilities, we're empowering users to focus on what matters most – driving results."
