## Data Centers Drive Surge in Natural Gas Power Plant Costs

Natural gas power plants have seen a staggering 66% increase in construction costs over the past two years, with build times now taking 23% longer. The reason behind this surge? An unprecedented rise in data center electricity demand.

### The Rise of Data Centers

As more businesses and individuals rely on cloud-based services to store and process vast amounts of data, data centers have become essential infrastructure for modern computing. These massive facilities require immense power to keep servers running 24/7, driving up demand for electricity.

### Powering the Cloud

With data center construction costs already high due to the need for robust cooling systems and secure storage solutions, natural gas power plants are now shouldering the burden of meeting this increased demand. As a result, costs have skyrocketed by 66%, making it more challenging for data centers to justify their electricity needs.
