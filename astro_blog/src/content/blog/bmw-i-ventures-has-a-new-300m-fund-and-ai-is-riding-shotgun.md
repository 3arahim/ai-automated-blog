## The Future of AI is Driving the Road Ahead
BMW i Ventures has announced a new $300 million fund to fuel the next generation of innovative technologies. This latest investment focuses on agentic AI, which enables machines to make decisions independently, as well as physical AI, a concept that blends AI with physical systems to create more efficient and responsive solutions.

The German automaker's venture capital arm is also looking at startups working on industrial software, advanced materials, and manufacturing and supply chain technologies. These areas are critical to the future of mobility, as they enable the development of sustainable and efficient vehicles, infrastructure, and logistics networks.
