## Apple Loses Bid to Pause App Store Fee Changes

In a significant development, Apple has lost its bid to pause the court-ordered changes to its App Store payment system. This decision keeps external purchase links in place as the case heads toward the Supreme Court. The dispute began when Epic Games, the creator of Fortnite, filed a lawsuit against Apple over its 30% commission on in-app purchases.

### Background: Apple's Commission Structure

Apple's commission structure has long been a point of contention among developers and gamers alike. The tech giant takes a 15% cut of in-app purchase revenue for games that use its App Store payment system, which can be significant considering the massive popularity of Fortnite. This prompted Epic Games to challenge the commission rate, arguing it stifles innovation and harms consumers.
