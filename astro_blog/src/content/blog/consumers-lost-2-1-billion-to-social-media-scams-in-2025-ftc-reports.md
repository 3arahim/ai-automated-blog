Consumers lost a whopping $2.1 billion to social media scams in 2025, reports the Federal Trade Commission (FTC). This marks an alarming eightfold increase from previous years, highlighting the growing threat of these types of fraudulent activities.

## The Rise of Social Media Scams

Social media platforms have become an increasingly popular channel for scammers to target unsuspecting consumers. According to the FTC's report, social media scams resulted in higher losses than any other method used by scammers to contact consumers.

### How Do Social Media Scams Work?

Scammers often create fake profiles or use existing ones to pose as friends, acquaintances, or even celebrities. They then attempt to trick victims into sharing sensitive information, such as login credentials, credit card numbers, or social security numbers. Other tactics include offering fake prizes, asking for donations, or convincing users to invest in fraudulent schemes.
