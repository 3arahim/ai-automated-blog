## Parallel Web Systems Hits $2B Valuation

Parallel Web Systems, the AI agent-tool startup founded by former Twitter CEO Parag Agrawal, has reached a significant milestone. Just five months after its last big raise, the company has secured another $100 million in funding, bringing its valuation to an impressive $2 billion.

### Funding and Future Plans

The latest round of funding was led by Sequoia, with participation from existing investors such as Founders Fund and Greylock Partners. The new capital will be used to accelerate Parallel Web Systems' mission to build the world's most advanced AI agent-tool platform.
